# Code of conduct

Agent Gateway lets an AI agent work inside a Foundry VTT world. Most people who use it run games built on other people's work: rulebooks, adventures, art and software. This code of conduct keeps the project respectful to the people in it and careful with that work. It applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commits and any other space run for this project.

It follows the spirit of the rules Foundry VTT sets for its own community and for the packages built on it.

## What we ask of everyone

1. **No pirated or paid content.** Do not share, link to or ask for cracked or leaked material. That covers premium modules, paid adventures, compendium content, Foundry VTT itself and licence keys. If you need help with a problem that involves paid content, describe it without attaching the content.
2. **Respect licences.** Game systems and modules are published under licences such as the ORC, the OGL or a publisher's own fan-content policy. Each system and each module has its own terms. Contributions here must stay within them, and you are responsible for knowing what applies to what you add.
3. **Do not paste copyrighted rulebook text.** In issues, pull requests and discussions, describe the rule in your own words or name the book and page. Short quotations that a licence clearly allows are the exception, and you should say which licence.
4. **Do not use the agent to take licensed content.** An AI agent connected through this project must not be used to extract, bulk-copy or redistribute licensed material: compendium packs, premium module content, paid art or similar. Bug reports and feature requests that exist mainly to make that easier will be closed.
5. **Behave decently.** Be respectful. No harassment, slurs, threats or targeted attacks. Do not post other people's personal data, and do not dig for it. Disagree with ideas, not with people. Keep to the topic of the project.

Please also keep secrets out of public posts: tokens, invite codes and pairing codes.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, reject contributions and block accounts that break these rules. In general the steps run in this order:

1. A reminder of the rule, in the thread or privately.
2. Removal of the offending content and a request not to repeat it.
3. A temporary block from the repository.
4. A permanent block, for serious or repeated breaches, or at once for harassment, threats or sharing pirated content.

Content that is clearly illegal or infringing is removed without a warning.

## Reporting a conduct problem

Use GitHub's "Report content" feature on the comment, issue or pull request, or contact the maintainer by their GitHub handle, `@AindriuB`. Reports are handled privately where possible, and the reporter's identity is not shared without consent.

A private vulnerability advisory is not the channel for conduct reports. Those are for security problems only; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope and changes

This applies to participation in the project's spaces and to anyone representing the project elsewhere. The maintainer may revise it as Foundry's policies or the project change.
